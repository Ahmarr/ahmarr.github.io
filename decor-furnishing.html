Overview

Furniture, shown where it lives.

Decor Furnishing & Mattresses is a showroom in Goregaon West, Mumbai. Its website is a catalogue of furniture and furnishings, photographed in the rooms where each piece was installed rather than staged.

The site is organised around how a visitor might browse: collections, individual furniture pieces, real spaces, and the showroom itself, with an enquiry page for anyone who has a piece in mind or a space to fill.

The website

What it includes.

Listed from the public website, as it stood in November 2026.

Collections

Furniture grouped by collection, such as living, bedrooms, and curtains and blinds.

Furniture catalogue

Individual pieces such as sofas, beds, headboards and a daybed. The furniture page lists 18 pieces (checked November 2026), each with a short material description.

Spaces

Rooms, details and work in progress, shown in context.

Showroom information

Address, opening hours and how to reach the showroom.

Enquiries

A dedicated enquiry page for visitors who want to ask about a piece or a room.

Careful descriptions

Materials are described as they appear (for example "appears woven fabric"), with anything that cannot be seen confirmed directly at enquiry.
