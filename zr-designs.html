Overview

Pieces that carry a story.

ZR Designs makes custom, made-to-order keychains and pendants in premium stainless steel, shaped around a name, a memory or an idea.

The storefront needed to do two jobs at once: let customers browse a ready-made range, and invite them to start something personal. Its home page leads with both paths: Explore the Collection and Create a Custom Piece.

Beyond keychains and pendants, the catalogue is organised into earrings, bracelets, chains, wallets and gifting. Supporting content explains how ordering works, why stainless steel suits everyday wear, and the answers to common questions.

  1. Choose

    Browse keychains, pendants, earrings and bracelets, or start something custom.

  2. Personalize

    Share a name, date, photo or idea.

  3. Craft

    Made to order in premium stainless steel.

  4. Deliver

    Shipped to the customer's door, ready to wear or gift.

The four steps above are the ordering journey as the website presents it to customers.

The storefront

What the website includes.

Features visible on the public website. This page does not describe the backend, payment integration, order handling or analytics, and makes no claim about how they were built.

Catalogue by category

Keychains, pendants, earrings, bracelets, chains, wallets and gifting, with collections for men, women and unisex pieces.

Search and filters

Products can be searched and filtered by category, gender and price, with a toggle to show customizable pieces only.

Custom orders

A dedicated custom-order path, linked from the home page and the navigation, for customers who want a personalised piece.

Accounts and cart

Customer sign-up and log-in, and a shopping cart.

Payment information

The site's FAQ states that payments are processed through Razorpay and that the shop does not see or store card, UPI or bank details. This is the site's own statement, not an independent check.

Trust and information

An FAQ, a "why stainless steel" section, the brand's story, and Terms, Privacy and Refund policy pages.
